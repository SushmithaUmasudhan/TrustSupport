TrustSupport LLM-as-Judge Rubric

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

1. Relevance
1 = Does not address the customer's issue.
3 = Partly addresses the issue but misses important details.
5 = Directly addresses the customer's actual issue.

2. Grounding
1 = Unsupported, invented, or unrelated information.
3 = Generally grounded but vague or only partly supported by the evidence.
5 = Clearly grounded in the retrieved historical response/evidence.

3. Helpfulness
1 = Gives no useful next step or resolution.
3 = Gives a partially useful next step.
5 = Gives a clear, actionable, appropriate next step.

4. Tone
1 = Inappropriate, rude, dismissive, or confusing.
3 = Acceptable but impersonal or awkward.
5 = Professional, empathetic, and clear.

5. Hallucination
1 = Contains major invented claims, promises, policies, or facts.
3 = Contains a minor unsupported claim or ambiguity.
5 = Contains no unsupported factual claim.

Overall score:
1 = Unacceptable
2 = Poor
3 = Adequate
4 = Good
5 = Strong

For each response, provide:
- Five dimension scores
- Overall score
- One short reason

Human agreement:
After LLM scoring, a human should independently score the same 20 examples using the same rubric. Compare LLM and human scores using exact agreement or mean absolute difference. Do not invent agreement numbers.
